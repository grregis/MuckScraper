Articles refused before anything is stored. Changes apply on the next run — no restart needed.

Not the same as the Scrape Blocklist. That one lists domains whose content won't scrape — those articles are still ingested and still count toward a story. These are never stored at all.

Matching is a plain case-insensitive substring, not a regex or an exact match. That is load-bearing for the league domains — nfl.com is what catches *-frontend.pocket.nfl.com, and nba.com also catches wnba.com — but it cuts both ways, so single common words make bad title keywords: picks would kill "Trump picks Supreme Court nominee". Untick Active to disable a rule without losing it.
